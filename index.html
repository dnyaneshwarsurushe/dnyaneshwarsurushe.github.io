⚖️ CAP & Consistency

Feeds & recommendations choose availability (AP); payments & auth stay strongly consistent (CP). The design follows the data, not dogma.

📈 Scaling Playbook

Cache first → read replicas → shard by user_id → move hot paths to events with Kafka. Scale the bottleneck, measure, repeat.

💸 Cost vs Latency

Semantic caching, model quantization, spot GPUs and autoscaling-to-zero for dev — production AI has to be affordable, not just accurate.
